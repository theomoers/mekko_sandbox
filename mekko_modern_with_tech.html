How to read the overlay

The hatched gray portion is the selected technology’s abatement potential for each subsector at the current horizon (2025 view = by 2035, 2035 view = by 2050, 2050 view = post-2050). Values come from the technology CSV, are displayed as a share of mapped reference-year emissions, and are always capped by each tile’s emissions.
