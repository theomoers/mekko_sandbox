How to read the overlay

The light hatched gray portion is independent abatement from selected technologies. The darker cross-hatched portion is incremental complementarity uplift (only when compatible technology pairs are selected and complementarities are enabled). Horizons map as 2025 view = by 2035, 2035 view = by 2050, 2050 view = post-2050. All overlays are capped by each tile’s emissions.
